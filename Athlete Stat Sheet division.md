Athlete Stat Sheet division

    1. A single File will contain a number of sub-sheets. 
    
        A. First: Profile. To this sheet, we will later on siphon the most important and interesting data about the given athlete. 
        
        B. Second: The Appearance Stats. We have just completed making. This Sheet is locked in for no. 
        
        C. Third: Events-based Stats. This one will purely take on event-based stats such as event wins, event win streaks, and so on. It will also be a first test at the normalization of WSM/Strongman events, which are notoriously hard to categorize 
        
        D. Fourth: Points-based Stats. This one will be more % based as the year-to-year variations in points are drastic in WSM competitions. 
        
        E. Fifth: Physical Stats. Centered around the athlete's physical attributes such as height weight, and age. 
        
        F. Sixth: Opponent Stats. This one will be centered around the stats of the opponents the Athlete Faced in their career, both individually as well as collectively
